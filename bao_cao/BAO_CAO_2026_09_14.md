## 3. PHAT HIEN 2 — NULL CUA ER PHAI LA DAO DAU

Dac ta §6.2 noi "sinh null bang block-bootstrap va GBM". Lam dung nhu vay thi
**G0 khong phat hien duoc gi**. Hai lan sai lien tiep, ca hai deu do duoc, tren
mot chuoi AR(+0,6) **dung san biet truoc dap an**:

| null | ac1 cua null | ER that | ER null | ket qua |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| block bootstrap thuan | ~0,60 | 0,2011 | 0,2006 | null giu luon tu tuong quan → **khong phan biet** |
| block + xao trong khoi | 0,03 | 0,2011 | 0,1910 | giet duoc ac1 nhung **giu trung binh khoi** → z = 0,7 |
| **dao dau ngau nhien** | ~0 | — | — | **phan biet duoc, p < 0,1** |

Ly do sau: ER do `|ΣΔP| / Σ|ΔP|`. Block bootstrap giu nguyen *trung binh cua khoi*,
ma trung binh khoi **chinh la** tu so cua ER khi khoi dai bang cua so. Null nuot
dung cai tin hieu dang do.

Dao dau giai ca hai cung luc va giai **chinh xac** chu khong xap xi: **mau so cua
ER khong doi mot chut nao** (giu nguyen `|ΔP|` dung tung vi tri thoi gian, nen cum
bien dong duoc bao toan nguyen ven), chi tu so bi giet. Phep thu thanh dung mot
cau hoi: *voi dung duong bien dong nay, cai thu tu dau ma thi truong da di co cho
ra net move khac ngau nhien khong?*

## 5. SAU CHO DAC TA NOI MOT DANG, TOAN DOI MOT NEO

### 5.1 Dieu kien G1 tach lam hai
Dac ta §3.1: *"`tp_dist` phai > `D_BE` + chi phi toan ro"*. Dung cho `anchor_return`
va `trail`, **sai cho `avg_plus`**: diem thoat cua `avg_plus` duoc dinh nghia TU
gia von binh quan, nen lai moi don vi luon bang dung `tp_dist` bat ke `D_BE` bao
nhieu. Ap nguyen van se **loai nham moi cau hinh DCA sau tang** — dung cai ma mau
tham chieu M2 dang chay that.

### 5.2 `anchor_return` + `WITH` khong bao gio lai
Voi `WITH` cac tang nam **phia sau** goc luoi theo huong vi the, nen "gia quay ve
goc" la mot khoan **lo chac chan**, khong phai TP. Loai o G1.

### 5.3 `max_legs` KHONG phai mot basket stop
Dac ta xep no vao bang §1.6 BASKET STOP. No la **tran phoi nhiem**: cham tran thi
ngung them tang, ro van mo. Cau hinh chi co `max_legs` la **martingale cho chet** —
dung thu §1.6 muon cam. G1 tu choi.

### 5.4 Ma dinh danh §8.7 KHONG duy nhat — ma dac ta bao dung no lam khoa dedupe

Chuoi cua §8.7 **khong phan biet duoc `tp_dist`** (cung nhu `size_r`, `step_g`).
Hai cau hinh khac han nhau cho ra y het mot ma. Ma §8.7 lai noi: *"Hash rut gon
tu chuoi nay lam khoa dedupe. Bat buoc, neu khong QuantLab se chay trung hang
nghin lan"*. Dung no lam khoa dedupe thi ta khong chay trung — ta **danh roi im
lang** moi bien the `tp_dist` tru mot cai.

Lo hong nay tu no lo ra: toi dung ma do de dung lai cau hinh song sot cua AUDCAD,
doan `tp_dist = 2,0` trong khi that ra la **1,0**, va ra mot ket qua khac han.

Da sua `ma_dinh_danh`. Khoa dedupe THAT van la `van_tay()` (hash toan bo
dataclass) nen kho chua he mat cau hinh nao — cho hong chi o chuoi cho NGUOI doc.

### 5.5 "Bi quan" ve thu tu khop KHONG co nghia la ket qua te hon

Ten `bi_quan` mo ta GIA DINH VE THU TU KHOP, khong mo ta ket qua, va chieu cua no
dao nguoc theo `direction` — voi `AGAINST` thi "cham cuc tri bat loi truoc" nghia
la khop sau hon o gia tot hon, tuc ban do LAI HON. Do duoc: +2,753% vs +0,199%,
chenh 14 lan, va ban ten "bi quan" la ban dep hon. Da them `lai_than_trong =
min(hai ban)`; cong `song` cua pheu gio doi ban than trong phai duong.

### 5.6 So bon ham SIZE phai CHUAN HOA DON BAY
Dac ta §1.4 hoi thang *"neu flat khong thang thi moi ham tang size chi la don bay
tra hinh"*, §7.3 doi bang so sanh bon ham o cung `h*`. Bang do chi tra loi duoc
neu bon ham chay o **cung phoi nhiem dinh**:

```
geometric r=2, n=12  ->  tong lot 4.095
flat n=12            ->  tong lot    12        (chenh 341 lan)
```

De nguyen thi ta dang so mot cau hinh don bay 341× voi mot cau hinh don bay 1×,
roi ket luan ve "hinh dang ham size". `sinh_cau_hinh` chia nguoc
`phoi_nhiem_1 = don_bay_dinh / Σq_k`.

(Do 14/09 tren EURGBP M5 khi chua chuan hoa: `flat` **chay tai khoan o −98%**. Do
khong phai ket luan ve co che, la ket luan ve co lot.)

```

```

GIA DINH KHOP (cham cuc tri bat loi truoc), khong noi ve ket qua, va chieu cua no

- `WITH`: cham cuc tri bat loi truoc = ro breakout bi can truoc khi kip nhoi them

Nen con so phai doc la **ban THAN TRONG = min(hai ban) = +0,199% / 13,5 nam =

Tuy gia dinh thu tu khop, no nam dau do giua **0,015%/nam va 0,20%/nam**. Moc
mua-giu AUDCAD cung ky la **−0,44%/nam** nen ky thuat ma noi thi PMG hon moc, nhung
hon chung do thi khong phai mot he giao dich - do la nhieu.

Mot chi tiet cua G3 dang giu lai: ban **dao dau** la ban YEU nhat (−0,06%) trong
khi **xao khoi** (−5,21%) va **GBM** (−9,64%) am han. Nghia la cai luoi nay khong
song bang drift — no song bang **cau truc chuoi** cua chinh AUDCAD, dung thu G0 da
chi ra. Do la ly do noi "co che that": no chi qua nho de dung.
