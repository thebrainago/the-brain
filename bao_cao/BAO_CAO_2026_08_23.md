Vi du that, doc tu `priceactionlab.com`:

> "The base case of the strategy is to go long the SPY ETF at the next open when
> the closing price crosses above the 200-day moving average **and to exit the
> trade at the next open when the closing price falls below the average**"

```json
{"ten": "mua_close_cheo_len_sma200", "ho": "xu_huong", "chieu": 1, "giu": 1,
 "vao": [{"trai": {"chi_bao":"gia","cot":"close"}, "phep": "cheo_len",
          "phai": {"chi_bao":"sma","n":200,"cot":"close"}}],
 "ra":  [{"trai": {"chi_bao":"gia","cot":"close"}, "phep": "<",
          "phai": {"chi_bao":"sma","n":200,"cot":"close"}}]}
```

### Nhung gi no CO Y khong lam
- **Khong doan nguong.** "Buy when RSI is oversold" -> tu choi. Doan nguong la
  bia ra mot con so roi kiem dinh chinh no — overfit truoc khi backtest bat dau.
- **Khong doc "or" thanh "va".** Ngu phap chi co VA; doc mot cau OR thanh VA la
  doc SAI, nguy hiem hon doc thieu vi ket qua trong nhu mot co che that.
- **Khong nhan muc gia tho so voi hang so.** "buy when the price is above 100"
  dung cu phap nhung 100 la muc gia cua mot ma tai mot nam.
- **Khong doc ma nguon.** Ma nguon co duong rieng; tha vao day thi mot dong
  `if (x < 30)` bat ky trong mot thu vien bat ky thanh mot "co che".

### Ba bay da sap that trong chinh phien nay (deu da khoa bang test)
1. **`<[^>]+>` an ca phep so sanh.** Bo the HTML bang bieu thuc do nuot tron
   `"< 5 ... the close >"` — tuc an dung nhung cau **dang gia nhat**, vi bai
   viet nao viet luat bang ky hieu deu co `<` va `>`.
2. **Khop ngan nuot khop dai.** "2-period RSI" va "RSI" cung khop; phep "lay
   cai cuoi cung ben trai" lay cai NGAN hon (bat dau muon hon) va cho ra
   `rsi n=14 mac dinh` **tu mot cau da noi ro n=2**. Doc mot con so co san
   thanh mot con so bia ra la loi nang hon ca khong doc duoc.
3. **Phan loai chieu tren CA cau.** "We open a **long** position when ... and
   close the position (or open a **short** position) when ..." — phan loai ca
   cau thi mot chien luoc MUA thanh chien luoc BAN.
