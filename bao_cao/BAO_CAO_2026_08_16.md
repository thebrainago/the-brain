## 3. CONG DA DUOC HIEU CHUAN HAI CHIEU — LAN DAU

`null_ty_le_lot` mot minh **vo nghia**: mot cong tu choi TAT CA cho ra so lieu y
het mot cong hieu chuan tot. Gio hai phep do chay cung nhau moi ngay.

```

  EURCAD H4 0/15 · AUDCAD H4 0/15 · EURGBP H4 0/15 · US500CASH D1 0/15
  --> 0/60 = 0,0%   (muc tieu <= 10%)

CHIEU 2 — BAI KIEM LUC (thu CO edge co qua khong)
  EURCAD    H4   50%->FAIL  55%->FAIL  60%->NGHI_NHIN_TRUOC   DAT
  AUDCAD    H4   50%->FAIL  55%->FAIL  60%->PASS              DAT
  US500CASH D1   50%->FAIL  55%->FAIL  60%->PASS              DAT
```

Nguong cua cong nam giua **do chinh xac 55% va 60%**. O 55% Sharpe con **-0,097**:
lat vi the moi bar thi chi phi an het. Do la con so dung, khong phai cong qua chat.

Bai kiem LUC nay chinh no cung tung sai va da duoc sua trong phien: ban dau
`p` duoc hieu la "ty le lan duoc mach nuoc, con lai boc ngau nhien" -> do chinh
xac that thanh `p + (1-p)/2`, nen `p=0,50` ra Sharpe **11,97** va bai kiem bao
"cong hong" trong khi cong khong he hong. Sai o **thiet ke bai kiem**, khong o
thu duoc kiem.

Placebo cung doi: them **null QUAY VONG** ben canh bootstrap khoi. Ly do — bootstrap
khoi bi KS-test bac bo tren phan lon ung vien that (`ks_p_min = 0,0000` tren
US500M H1), va khi bi bac bo thi cong danh truot dieu kien 5, tuc chien luoc bi
loai vi **chat luong cua null** chu khong vi khong co edge. Quay vong khong the bi
KS bac bo theo cau tao: no giu y nguyen phoi nhiem, do dai tung doan giu, so lan
vao lenh va phan phoi khoang cach giua cac lan vao; thu duy nhat no pha la su
trung khop giua thoi diem va gia — dung cai ta muon kiem.

Canary + mutation audit chay lai sau moi thay doi: **5/5 dat, bay van nhay 4/4**.
