## 1. Khau DU LIEU — chon ban theo DO PHU, khong theo byte

### 1.1 Sua

`du_lieu.kho()` gio giu **moi ban** cua mot ma (`cac_ban`). `nap(ma, khung)`
goi `ban_cho_khung(ma, khung)` de chon:

1. do phu o dung khung do (`_uoc_bar_o_khung`, tu choi ban co khung goc LON hon
   khung yeu cau — noi suy nguoc la che ra bar khong ton tai);
2. trong pham vi 90% do phu tot nhat thi uu tien ban **co cot `spread`**
   (mat cot spread la tut do tin chi phi tu SAN xuong KHAI);
3. roi den ban dung khung goc, roi file to hon, roi ten (de tat dinh).

Ten cache doi thanh `{MA}_{KHUNG}__{ten_file_nguon}.parquet`. Ten cu chi mang
`{MA}_{KHUNG}` nen khi ban duoc chon doi, cache cu van "moi hon file goc" va
duoc dung tiep — tuc doi nguon xong van doc du lieu cu. 222 file cache cu
(77 MB) da chuyen sang `lab/backups/data_khung_cu_20260824/`.

### 1.2 Hai bay da do TRUOC khi sua, va ca hai deu that

Neu chi lay "ban D1 nao nhieu bar nhat" thi hai chuoi sau se lot vao:

- **`us500cash_daily.parquet` KHONG phai CFD.** No khop **0,000** voi Yahoo
  ^GSPC (`sp500_daily`) va lech 0,073% voi CFD cua XM; ngay 16/03/2020 no mo o
  2508,59 con XM mo o 2675,20. Lay no lam ban D1 cua US500CASH la vua doi cong
  cu vua **nhan ban SP500 thanh hai tai san** trong cung ho FDR.
- **`AUDCAD_daily_dai` / `AUDNZD_daily_dai` / `NZDCAD_daily_dai`** cung muc gia
  voi ban broker (lech 0,24-0,36%) nhung **tuong quan loi suat ngay chi
  0,08-0,14** — nen ngay cat o gio khac. Ghep hai chuoi nay la ghep hai chuoi
  khac nhau.

Luat da cai (`lop_nguon_cua`): **mot ma co ban `san` thi moi ban `ngoai` mang
cung ten deu bi loai.** Nguoc lai, `xm_us500cash_daily` va `us500cash_xm_daily`
lech **0,0000%** va tuong quan **1,0000** — cung mot chuoi — nen `nguon_cua`
duoc sua de xep tien to `xm_` vao `san` (truoc do mot ban ket thuc bang `_daily`
bi xep `ngoai`, tuc cung mot cong cu ma bi ap hai nguong open bia khac nhau).

### 1.3 Ket qua do duoc

**21 ma dai ra, 4 ma "ngan di" nhung KHONG mat nam nao** — bon ma do chi mat
nhung bar Chu nhat cut do `resample` sinh ra (EURUSD 15.761 -> 14.322 bar tren
cung khoang 1971-2026: 284 bar/nam xuong 258 bar/nam, tuc dung so ngay giao dich
that). O H1 va H4 chi US500CASH doi: **+7 nam** (2018 -> 2011) o ca hai khung.

Kiem lai an toan: 13 chuoi `xm_*` doi lop nguon deu co ty le
`open[i]==close[i-1]` cao nhat **theo nam** la 0,46 (nam 2017 cua vai cap FX) —
khong chuoi nao cham nguong 0,60, tuc khong doan nao thuoc dien "gia bia" duoc
tha qua.

## 3. Khau SO FDR — bo do luc tu lam hong cai thuoc no dang doc

Day la loi nang nhat cua phien.

`do_luc._mot_muc` chay mot **oracle tong hop** (tin hieu biet truoc voi do chinh
xac `p`) qua `cong.xet(tren_holdout=True)` — va duong do **GHI mot dong quyet
dinh vao so `fdr`**. Do that: mot vong quet be mat viet **~80 dong/phut**
(174 dong trong 2 phut dau) vao ho `family=do_luc`. Ho do da co **795 dong**
tinh den 24/08 — **tat ca deu la do dac, khong dong nao la mot gia thuyet ve
thi truong.**

Nguong LORD giam theo `1/j^1.6`. Tuc **cang do luc nhieu thi nguong cang chat**:
bo do tu lam hong cai thuoc no dang doc.

Ho `do_luc` la mot epoch rieng nen no khong an vao ngan sach cua ho gia thuyet
that — thiet ke epoch da chan duoc phan thiet hai nang nhat. Nhung mot so quyet
dinh co 795 dong khong phai quyet dinh nao thi khong ai doc duoc, va cai gia
phai tra la **khong biet ngan sach that con bao nhieu**.

Da sua: `cong.lord_v2` / `cong.lord` / `cong.xet` nhan `ghi_so`. `ghi_so=False`
van tinh **dung** nguong ma phep thu that se gap (dung vi tri `j`, dung cac
reject truoc do) nhung **khong chen dong nao**. `do_luc._mot_muc` goi voi
`ghi_so=False`.

Do lai sau khi sua: mot lan `duong_cong_luc` = **0 dong moi**; ba file test
tung ghi vao so (`test_sang_loc`, `test_nha_may_luc`, `test_cong_do_phan_giai`)
gio deu **+0**. Truoc khi sua, hai lan chay `pytest` da them 28 dong that vao
so quyet dinh — tuc **chinh bo test cung dang tieu ngan sach**.

**Chua lam, cho nguoi dung quyet:** 795 dong `family=do_luc` co tu 21-22/08 van
con trong so. Xoa dong khoi mot so kiem toan la viec khong the hoan tac, nen
toi khong tu lam. De nghi: giu nguyen va danh dau, hoac chuyen sang mot bang
`do_dac` rieng.
