## B1. Quyet dinh: cong KHA THI, khong phai lenh cam theo lop tai san

Cau hoi la: 33 cap FX 13 nam co nguong phat hien 1,39, moi cap dang ky 3 gia
thuyet moi vong = gan 100 suat FDR tieu o cho khong the thang. Co nen ngung
dang ky khong?

**Khong chan theo lop tai san.** Chan "FX" la mot khang dinh ve thi truong ma ta
chua chung minh, va no se chan luon nhung cap FX du manh.

**Chan theo KHA THI cua tung ung vien** (`do_luc.du_luc_de_kiem`):

> Mot ung vien co Sharpe tren tap kham pha THAP HON MDE cua chinh cap do thi
> confirmation khong the phan xu duoc no — no se FAIL du gia thuyet dung hay
> sai. Chay no van ton mot suat FDR, va suat do lam CAO NGUONG cho moi phep
> thu sau (LORD giam theo 1/j^1.6).

Ba tinh chat khien quy tac nay an toan:
- **Cuc bo.** Khong noi gi ve lop tai san; ung vien FX du manh di tiep binh thuong.
- **Than trong dung chieu.** Sharpe tren tap kham pha la con so DA duoc chon loc
  (tot nhat trong ~65 to hop) nen no lac quan. Doi hoi no vuot MDE la mot bai
  kiem DE; thu nao truot bai de thi chac chan khong qua duoc bai kho.
- **Khong im lang.** So ung vien bi chan ghi vao chi so `kham_pha_thieu_luc`.

Kem theo: cap co `MDE = None` (khong muc do chinh xac nao toi 0,70 vuot duoc
cong) bi **bo qua ngay tu dau vong quet** — quet no chi de sinh ra ung vien
chac chan bi chan ngay sau do.
