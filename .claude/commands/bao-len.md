Dung lenh `b cau noi` (Windows: `.\b.cmd cau noi "<noi dung>"`; Linux: `python3 b.py cau noi "<noi dung>"`) de gui
NGUYEN VAN noi dung duoi day len phien Claude tren cloud. Khong tu y sua, them hay tom tat. Nho escape dau nhay
cho dung shell dang dung.

$ARGUMENTS

Sau khi chay: in `id` cua thu va cho biet `danh_thuc.da_goi` la true hay false. Neu false thi noi ly do (thieu lenh
`claude`? chua `b cau dat-session session_XXXX`?). Neu co `loi_git` thi thu da ghi o local nhung chua len git: bao
chu du an. Thu tra loi cua cloud se tu hien o cau ke tiep chu du an go (hook) hoac go `/thu`.
